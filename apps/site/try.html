What this page is, and is not

  • It runs two real parts of Kakurega in your browser: the secret detector and the checked, encrypted route to NEAR AI Cloud. The product itself reviews whole projects in a sandbox, proves each finding with a script and releases signed capsules; this page does none of that.
  • A free try goes through a relay of ours that adds our NEAR AI key. It passes on only a question sealed in your browser to the model's key, so it has no way to read one. It allows one model, short answers and a few tries a day from one address; the true ceiling is the small balance of that key. With your own key the page talks to NEAR AI Cloud directly.
  • A browser cannot check the identity of the connection itself the way the desktop does. The question is still encrypted to the key the hardware proof vouches for.
  • The detector knows the shapes of real keys, whole private-key blocks, and passwords said as passwords ("the password is ..."). A password made of ordinary words with nothing saying it is one is not recognised.
