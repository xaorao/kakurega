What the detector does not catch

  • It knows the shapes of real keys and tokens, whole private-key blocks, and passwords said as passwords ("the password is ..."). It is a heuristic, and it is not complete.
  • A password made of ordinary words ("the password is purple elephant", or one plain word shorter than sixteen letters) is not recognised: it reads like any sentence.
  • A password or a code said with no word for what it is ("type Blue42 when it asks") is not recognised, and is sent like any other line. These are the misses the benchmark names.
  • A wallet recovery phrase is recognised as twelve or more words in a row from the standard English list; one in another language, split over lines, or with other words mixed in is not.
  • This page runs the line-by-line part of the gate, as the try-it page does. In the app, files that hold secrets are also withheld whole and planted instructions are marked; neither is shown here.
  • Whether your secret got through is judged as the benchmark judges a leak: the whole secret, each half of one of 16 characters or more, and each of those as written, in base64, in hex or URL-encoded. A secret changed any other way, or cut into smaller pieces, is not seen by this check.
  • The page sends nothing you type. By itself it asks GitHub's public API for the count of reports; that request carries nothing from the page. The full list of limits is docs/LIMITS.md.
